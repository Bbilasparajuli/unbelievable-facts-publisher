Data Deletion Instructions

This page explains how the authorized operator can revoke platform access and request deletion of data associated with Unbelievable Facts Publisher.

YouTube / Google

  1. Open your Google Account security settings.
  2. Review third-party connections or account permissions.
  3. Locate the authorization associated with Unbelievable Facts Publisher and revoke access.

Google account permissions

Facebook / Meta

  1. Open Facebook Settings and the current Apps and Websites / connected-app management area.
  2. Locate the app associated with Unbelievable Facts Publisher.
  3. Remove or revoke the app's access.

Request deletion

After revocation, the Publisher will delete retained authorized platform data that is no longer required for the disclosed operational purpose as soon as reasonably possible, subject to applicable legal obligations.

You may also request deletion directly by emailing: bhaktibparajuli@gmail.com

Please identify the platform involved (YouTube/Google or Facebook/Meta) and the relevant channel or Page so the request can be located and completed.
