Unbelievable Facts Publisher

Unbelievable Facts Publisher is a private creator-operated automation tool used by a single authorized content creator to prepare and publish original short-form informational and entertainment videos to the creator's own social-media channels.

Supported publishing integrations

YouTube: The Publisher uses YouTube API Services to authenticate the authorized channel owner, verify the destination channel, upload creator-owned videos, set creator-approved titles, descriptions and visibility, and confirm upload results.

Facebook / Meta: The Publisher is designed to use Meta Platform APIs, when enabled and authorized by the operator, to publish creator-owned videos to the operator's own Facebook Page, including supported Reel and Story publishing flows.

Access model

The Publisher is not offered as a public multi-user service. It is operated only by the authorized creator/channel or Page owner through a private automation workflow.

User control

The authorized operator controls the media to be published and the relevant metadata and publishing settings supported by each platform. The Publisher does not request or store a user's YouTube, Google, or Facebook password.
